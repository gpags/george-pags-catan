What's the commission?

We'll agree it with you when we set your link up, and it's the same on a $99 template as it is on a $349 commission. We'd rather talk about it directly than post a number that turns out to be wrong for how you actually work.

Do I have to buy anything or hold stock?

No. There's nothing to buy in, nothing to store and nothing to ship. Your clients order from us and we make and send the piece.

Can I give the gift to more than one client?

Yes — it applies to every client's first scratcher, not to the first client who uses your link. It's a welcome gift, not a one-off coupon.

What if my client's cat has passed away?

We don't take memorial commissions at the moment. It deserves more care than we can currently promise while we're two people learning how long everything takes, and we'd rather say so than handle it badly.
