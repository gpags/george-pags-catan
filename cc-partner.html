What’s the margin?

We agree it with you when we set your account up, and it’s the same on a $59 template as on a $279 commission. We’d rather talk about it directly than post a number that turns out to be wrong for how you actually work.

Do I have to buy stock or hold inventory?

No. Nothing to buy in, nothing to store, nothing to ship. Orders come to us and we make and send the piece.

Can a shelter take the margin as a donation?

Yes. Tell us on the form and we’ll set it up so the margin goes back to the shelter rather than to an individual.

What if a client’s cat has passed away?

We don’t take memorial commissions at the moment. It deserves more care than we can promise while we’re two people still learning how long everything takes, and we’d rather say so than handle it badly.
