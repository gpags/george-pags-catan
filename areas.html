Service areas

One county. Covered properly.

We serve Somerset County, New Jersey — every township and borough in it — and we stay inside it. Working one county instead of half the state is what makes unhurried, on-time visits possible.

Why we stay inside one county

A sitter juggling half of New Jersey is a sitter who is always a little behind. Visits get trimmed, arrival windows widen, and the cat waiting at the end of the route gets the worst of it. Keeping to one county means short drives between homes, real 30-minute visits, and the same person showing up on time. One of us grew up in Basking Ridge, so these are roads we actually know — including which ones flood and which ones you don’t want to take in the snow.

Cat sitting in Basking Ridge, NJ

Home base, and the shortest drive on the list. Basking Ridge is where one of us grew up, so everything from the older homes near the village to the newer developments off Lyons Road is familiar territory. These visits get the tightest arrival windows we offer.

Cat sitting in Bernardsville, NJ

Including the quieter back roads and the properties set well off the street — the ones that make a rushed sitter cut a visit short. We build the extra minutes into the route instead.

Cat sitting in Bedminster, NJ

Single-family homes and condo communities alike, including the townhome clusters around The Hills. Gate codes, fobs and front-desk sign-ins get sorted at the Meet & Greet so nothing is improvised on day one.

Cat sitting in Warren, NJ

Warren Township homes tend to sit on larger lots with longer driveways, which suits indoor cats who like a quiet house and a predictable routine.

Cat sitting in Watchung, NJ

A short hop from Warren, so twice-daily visits stay practical here — useful for cats on medication schedules.

Cat sitting in Bridgewater, NJ

One of the county’s largest townships, and a regular stop on our routes. Plenty of two-cat and three-cat households.

Cat sitting in Branchburg, NJ

Quieter, more spread out, and easy to reach outside rush hour. Good coverage for longer trips.

Cat sitting in Somerville, NJ

The county seat, with a mix of older homes and newer apartments. Building access is straightforward once we have it on file.

Cat sitting in Raritan, NJ

Right next to Somerville and covered on the same route, which keeps arrival windows tight.

Cat sitting in Hillsborough, NJ

A big township with a lot of families who travel — and a lot of cats who would rather stay home than board.

Cat sitting in Montgomery, NJ

Toward the southern end of the county. Best booked with a little notice so we can plan the route around it.

Cat sitting in Franklin, NJ

Including Somerset and the surrounding neighborhoods. Apartment and townhouse access is no problem.

Cat sitting in Far Hills, NJ

Small, quiet and close to home base. An easy addition to any morning route.

Cat sitting in Peapack-Gladstone, NJ

Neighboring Far Hills and equally close. Properties here often sit back from the road, and we plan for that.

Cat sitting in Green Brook, NJ

On the eastern edge of the county and reachable without a long haul.

Cat sitting in North Plainfield, NJ

Denser housing and street parking — we work it into the schedule so visits still run the full 30 minutes.

Cat sitting in Bound Brook, NJ

Covered alongside South Bound Brook and Manville on the same run.

Cat sitting in South Bound Brook, NJ

Small borough, short drive, no trouble fitting it in.

Cat sitting in Manville, NJ

Straightforward residential streets and easy access. A reliable stop on the southern route.

Cat sitting in Millstone, NJ

One of the smallest boroughs in the state, and one of the quickest visits on our map.

Cat sitting in Rocky Hill, NJ

At the far southern tip of the county. Best for multi-day bookings so the drive earns its place.

Cat sitting in Gillette, NJ

Just over the county line in Long Hill Township, Morris County — we serve it anyway because it is a short drive from home base.

What if I'm just outside those five towns?

Tell us anyway. Choose “Other” on the booking form and add your town — if we can reach you without rushing the rest of the route, we will. If we can't, we'll say so plainly rather than overpromise and let you down mid-trip. Either way you'll get a straight answer, usually the same day.
