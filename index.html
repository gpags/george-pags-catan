Common questions

Will it look exactly like my cat?

Not exactly. Here is the honest answer.

  • We paint the 3D Model in software.
  • We match it the best we can to your cats pattern.
  • We simplify some patterns to match the art capabilities.
  • Colors and markings come through. Fine detail does not.
When do I send my photo?

After you order. Nothing to upload before you pay. We email you, and you reply with your photo.

Send a clear photo and the name you want (if applicable). Your order waits until your photo arrives.

How long does it take?
  • We ship it out as fast as we can, typically takes 1–2 days to get ready once your photo arrives and ships in 3–5 business days
  • We keep you posted the whole way: photo and video updates of your actual piece, emailed to you as it’s made
What if my cat ignores it?

Put it where they already scratch. Next to the couch or on rugs works best. Message us anyway and we will help the best we can

How do refills work?

The pad lifts out and a new one drops in. Most cats need one every 2 months but we know owners typically do it every 6 months.

for one. for three. for six.

What if it breaks?
  • The cardboard is meant to get shredded. That is the job.
  • Damaged on arrival? Tell us within 7 days and we’ll work with you.
  • A part fails in 30 days? Also covered.
