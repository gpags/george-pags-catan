Common questions

Does it have my cat on it?

No. The sleeping cat is part of the design and is the same on every one. For your own cat, drawn from your photo with their name, look at Cottage Kitties.

Can I have it in another colour?

Not yet. Sleepy Kitty is made in sage only. Cottage Kitties comes in four.

How long does it take?

5–10 business days to build, plus shipping. Nothing is made ahead of time.

Does it take the same pads?

Yes. Every CatCustoms frame takes the same cardboard pad, on purpose.

What if it breaks?

The cardboard is meant to get shredded — that is the consumable part. Damaged on arrival? Tell us within 7 days and we replace it. A part fails in 30 days? Also covered.
