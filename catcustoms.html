How a template order works

Three things to do. Only one of them happens before you pay.

1

Pick your design

Cottage, Bamboo, Castle or Bloom. They're the same size and take the same insert — the difference is entirely how they look in your room.

2

Pick a colourway

Three per design. We print and hand-finish in the one you choose, so the piece arrives in the colours you actually picked.

3

Send a photo — after checkout

Nothing to upload before you pay. Order first, then send a clear photo of your cat and the name you want on the front.

📮 Why after? Because a photo upload is the single most common place a cat-shaped order gets abandoned. Buy it in thirty seconds, send the picture when you've got a good one — we'll email you the link and wait.
The personalised parts

The frame is the design. Your cat is the part we make.

Every scratcher is built from a printed frame plus three pieces made specifically for your order.

  • 1A figurine of each cat. Sculpted to your photo, hand-painted to their colouring and markings, then set into the windows.
  • 2A nameplate. Their name, raised and painted, on the front of the piece — not a sticker, not an engraving.
  • 3A corrugated insert. The bit your cat actually destroys. It lifts out and a fresh one drops in for $12.

On likeness: your cat is sculpted, not scanned. The style keeps what makes them recognisable — the markings, the colouring, the face — and lets the rest go. It's a portrait the way a good tattoo is a portrait. Not a copy. A likeness.

Before you order

The things people actually ask.

Will it look exactly like my cat?

No — and that's deliberate. Your cat is sculpted and hand-painted, not scanned. The style keeps what makes them recognisable — the markings, the colouring, the shape of the face — and lets the rest go. It's a portrait the way a good tattoo is a portrait. Not a copy. A likeness.

When do I send my photo?

After you order. There's nothing to upload before you pay — we email you a link, and you send a clear photo of your cat plus the name you'd like on the front whenever you've got a good one.

How long does it take?

A template is 5–10 business days to make, plus transit. Nothing is made before you order.

A 1-of-1 takes longer, because roughly a working week goes into one and only three are taken each month. You'll get a real date when you order, not an estimate we quietly extend.

What if I don't like the concepts?

Then they aren't finished. Concepts exist so you can say no to them — that's the entire job of a concept. Nothing gets modelled, printed or painted off a design you merely tolerated.

What if my cat ignores it?

Cats have opinions about placement more than they have opinions about scratchers. Put it where they already scratch — beside the sofa arm, across a doorway they use, next to where they sleep — rather than where you'd prefer it lived.

Because every piece is made for one specific cat, we can't resell it, so it isn't returnable for a change of mind. Message us anyway if it isn't working; we've usually got a suggestion.

How do refills work?

The insert lifts out and a new one drops in. Nothing is glued, nothing needs tools, and the frame, figurines and nameplate are untouched by the swap. $12 for one, $17 for two, $44 for four kept on hand.

What if it gets destroyed?

The cardboard is supposed to get destroyed; that's the part doing the work, and it costs $12 to undo. The frame, the figurines and the nameplate all sit outside the wear surface.

If it arrives damaged, tell us within 7 days and we'll replace it. If a part fails from a material defect within 30 days, that's covered too. A dropped corner isn't — but message us, because a single part is usually quick to reprint.
