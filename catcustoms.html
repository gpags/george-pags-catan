The frame is the design. Your cat is the part we make.

Before you order

The things people actually ask.

Will it look exactly like my cat?

Not exactly, no — and that’s the honest answer. We build the 3D model with AI from the photo you send, then rework it by hand in Blender until it looks right. The design gets simplified on purpose so it matches the art style of the piece: chunky shapes, clean lines, no fur detail.

What you get is a likeness, not a replica. The colouring and the markings people recognise your cat by come through. The fine detail deliberately doesn’t.

When do I send my photo?

After you order. There’s nothing to upload before you pay — we email you a link, and you send a clear photo of your cat plus the name for the front whenever you’ve got a good one.

How long does it take?

A template is 5–10 business days to make, plus transit. Nothing is made before you order, and the clock starts when your photo arrives.

A 1-of-1 takes longer, because roughly a working week goes into one and only three are taken each month.

What if my cat ignores it?

Cats have opinions about placement more than they have opinions about scratchers. Put it where they already scratch — beside the sofa arm, across a doorway they use, next to where they sleep.

Because every piece is made for one specific cat, we can’t resell it, so it isn’t returnable for a change of mind. Message us anyway if it isn’t working; we’ve usually got a suggestion.

How do refills work?

The insert lifts out and a new one drops in — nothing is glued and nothing needs tools. Most cats go through one about every two months. $10 for one, $25 for three, $40 for six, and 10% off any of those on a subscription.

What if it gets destroyed?

The cardboard is supposed to get destroyed; that’s the part doing the work. The frame, the figures and the nameplate all sit outside the wear surface.

If it arrives damaged, tell us within 7 days and we’ll replace it. If a part fails from a material defect within 30 days, that’s covered too.
