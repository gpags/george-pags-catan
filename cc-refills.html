Most cat scratchers need replacement every 2 months.

Yours doesn’t — only the cardboard does. Set up a refill plan and the next insert turns up before your cat has finished the one they’re on. Cancel or skip whenever you like.

🔁 Skip or cancel whenever. Cats do not read schedules. If yours has barely touched the last one, skip the delivery — we would rather you had an empty cupboard than a stack of cardboard you never opened.

How often will I actually need one?

2

Every two months, roughly

That’s the number we plan around, and it’s what most scratchers of this size need under normal use.

?

But cats vary enormously

Some go through one in a month. Some barely touch it. Yours will settle into its own rhythm within the first couple of inserts.

→

So the plan bends

Skip a delivery, bring one forward, or cancel outright. The schedule exists to save you thinking about it, not to lock you in.

Do inserts fit every design?

Yes. Every CatCustoms frame — every template and every 1-of-1 — takes the same insert, on purpose. One spare works with whatever you own now and whatever you buy later.

How do I know when to replace it?

When your cat stops using it, or when the corrugation has been shredded past the surface and the flutes are collapsing. There’s no harm in running one right down — the frame underneath isn’t part of the wear surface.

Can I skip a delivery?

Yes, any time, and it doesn’t cost you the discount. Skipping pushes the next one back rather than cancelling it.

Can I recycle the old one?

It’s corrugated cardboard, so yes — flatten it and put it in with your paper recycling. Shake the loose bits out first unless you enjoy sweeping.
