The Craftsman’s Journey

Nine stages, start to finish. Every one is a real email with real pictures attached, and nothing moves on until you’ve said yes to where it is.

1You send

A photo of your cat

Any decent picture. Asleep on a towel is perfect — we’re after markings, colouring and the shape of the face.

2We send

Concepts, for approval

A spread of directions — different settings, framings and moods. Deliberately unfinished. You tell us which way to go.

3We send

The tuned design

One direction, refined until it’s right. Colours, proportions, where each cat sits. Last stop before anything is built.

4We build

It becomes a 3D model

AI builds the first pass from the approved design, then we rework it by hand in Blender until the shapes are right.

5We build

It gets prepped to print

Walls thickened, overhangs solved, the insert channel cut to depth, the whole thing split into parts that print cleanly.

6We build

It gets printed

Frame, cat figures and nameplate come off the printers as separate pieces. You get a photo of the parts on the plate.

7We build

It gets painted by hand

Your cat’s colouring and the markings you’d recognise from across a room. The stage that takes longest.

8We send

It gets boxed and shipped

A photo of the finished piece before it’s wrapped, then a tracking number. You know it’s coming.

9You

You open it

The insert goes in, the cats go in the windows, and it goes wherever your cat already scratches.

Then you tell everyone.
