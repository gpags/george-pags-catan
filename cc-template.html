How a template order works

Three things to do. Only two of them happen before you pay.

1

Pick your colourway

Four to choose from. We print and hand-finish in the one you pick, so the piece arrives in the colours you actually chose.

2

Type their name

Up to 18 characters, raised and painted on the sign at the front. You confirm the spelling before anything is made.

3

Send a photo — after checkout

Nothing to upload before you pay. Order first, then send a clear photo of your cat whenever you’ve got a good one.

📮 Why after? Because a photo upload is the single most common place a cat-shaped order gets abandoned. Buy it in thirty seconds, send the picture when you’ve got a good one — we’ll email you the link and wait.

Before you order

Will it look exactly like my cat?

Not exactly, no — and that’s the honest answer. We build the 3D model with AI from the photo you send, then rework it by hand in Blender until it looks right. The design gets simplified on purpose so it matches the art style of the piece: chunky shapes, clean lines, no fur detail.

What you get is a likeness, not a replica. The colouring and the markings people recognise your cat by come through. The fine detail deliberately doesn’t.

Can I have two cats on it?

Yes. The Cottage has two windows and comes with two cat figures as standard, so a pair is the normal case rather than an extra. One cat is fine too — we’ll talk to you about what goes in the second window.

When do I send my photo?

After you order. We email you a link the moment your payment clears. Send a clear, well-lit picture — asleep on a blanket is perfect — plus the name for the sign.

How long does it take?

5–10 business days to make, plus transit. Nothing is made before you order. The clock starts when your photo arrives, not when you pay.

What if it gets destroyed?

The cardboard is supposed to get destroyed; that’s the part doing the work. The frame, the figures and the nameplate all sit outside the wear surface.

If it arrives damaged, tell us within 7 days and we’ll replace it. If a part fails from a material defect within 30 days, that’s covered too.
