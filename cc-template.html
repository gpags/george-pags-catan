Before you order

Will it look exactly like my cat?

No — and that's deliberate. Your cat is sculpted and hand-painted, not scanned. The style keeps what makes them recognisable — the markings, the colouring, the shape of the face — and lets the rest go. It's a portrait the way a good tattoo is a portrait. Not a copy. A likeness.

When do I send my photo?

After you order. We email you a link the moment your payment clears. Send a clear, well-lit picture — asleep on a blanket is perfect — plus the name you'd like on the sign.

Can I have two cats on it?

Yes. The Cottage has two windows and comes with two figurines as standard, so a pair is the normal case rather than an extra. One cat is fine too — we'll talk to you about what goes in the second window.

How long does it take?

5–10 business days to make, plus transit. Nothing is made before you order. The clock starts when your photo arrives, not when you pay — so the sooner you send it, the sooner it ships.

What if it gets destroyed?

The cardboard is supposed to get destroyed; that's the part doing the work, and it costs $12 to undo. The frame, the figurines and the nameplate all sit outside the wear surface.

If it arrives damaged, tell us within 7 days and we'll replace it. If a part fails from a material defect within 30 days, that's covered too.
