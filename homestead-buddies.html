Common questions

Will it look exactly like my cats?

Not exactly. Here is the honest answer.

  • We paint the 3D model in software.
  • We match it the best we can to your cats’ patterns.
  • We simplify some patterns to match the art capabilities.
  • Colors and markings come through. Fine detail does not.
Only have one cat?

That's fine — Basking Paws is the one-cat version of the same design. Or order Homestead Buddies and tell us what you'd like in the second spot.

When do I send my photos?

After you order. We email you as soon as you pay — reply with a photo of each cat and the names you want. Your order waits until they arrive.

Asleep on a blanket is a great photo. Bright light helps.

How long does it take?

1–2 days to make once your photos arrive, then 3–5 business days in the mail. We email you photo and video updates along the way.

What if it breaks?
  • The cardboard is meant to get shredded.
  • Damaged on arrival? Tell us in 7 days and we’ll work with you.
  • A part fails in 30 days? Also covered.
