What counts as a post

Simple rules, so nobody has to guess.

  • ✓Any public account. Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads or X.
  • ✓Your scratcher is in it. Your cat on it is even better.
  • ✓Tag us or name us. @realizedprints, or write mycatscratcher.com.
  • ✓Your code is in the caption once you have it.
  • ✓Say it’s a partnership. Add #ad or #partner. It’s an FTC rule, and it keeps your account safe.
  • ✓5 different days, all within 60 days of joining.
  • ✓Stories count. They vanish in 24 hours, so send us a screenshot.
  • ✓Leave them up for at least 30 days.
  • ✕No private accounts and no posting the same video 5 times.
  • ✕No paid ads with our name and no coupon sites. Share it with real people.

Do I need a lot of followers?

No. Your friends and family trust you more than any ad. A post seen by 200 people who know you sells better than one seen by 20,000 strangers.

How does my code work?

Your friend types your code at checkout and gets $10 off any order with a scratcher in it. It stacks with our multi-scratcher deals. You get $10 for every one of those orders.

When do I get paid?

On the 1st of each month, for the orders from the month before, by PayPal, Venmo or store credit. Cancelled or refunded orders don’t count.

Can I do both the posts and the intro?

The pads are one pack per person. Pick whichever is easier. Your code keeps earning either way, and there is no limit on that.

Can I use my own code?

No, it’s for the people you share it with. Want more pads for yourself? They’re on the refills page.
