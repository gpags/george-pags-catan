Common questions

Can I have my own cat on it?

Yes. Pick “My cat” under Color. Right after checkout, upload a photo on your order page, or reply to your confirmation email with it, and we paint the cat to match. Basking Paws has no nameplate.

What if my cat isn't one of the six coats?

Choose “My cat” and send us a photo. We match your cat’s colors and markings as closely as the art style allows, and simplify complex patterns. Only Tuxedo has a real photo so far — the other coats show a drawn preview.

How long does it take?

1–2 business days to make (once your photo arrives, if we’re matching your cat), then 3–5 business days in the mail. We email you photo and video updates along the way.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Tick “This is a gift”, add a note if you like, and use their address at checkout. We leave prices and the receipt out of the box.

Does it take the same pads?

Yes. Every Cat Scratchers frame takes the same cardboard pad, on purpose.

What if it breaks?

The cardboard is meant to get shredded — that is the consumable part. Damaged on arrival? Tell us within 7 days and we replace it. A part fails in 30 days? Also covered. Details.
