Common questions

Can I have my own cat on it, with their name?

Your own cat, yes. We paint the cat to match the photo you send after ordering. A name, no — Basking Paws has no nameplate. For names, see Homestead Buddies.

What if my cat isn't one of the six coats?

That's fine. The six coats are just a starting point for the preview. We match the final pattern to your photo, and simplify where the art style needs it. Only Tuxedo has a real photo so far — the others show a drawn stand-in.

How long does it take?

1–2 days to make once your photo arrives, then 3–5 business days in the mail. We email you photo and video updates along the way.

Does it take the same pads?

Yes. Every Cat Scratchers frame takes the same cardboard pad, on purpose.

What if it breaks?

The cardboard is meant to get shredded — that is the consumable part. Damaged on arrival? Tell us within 7 days and we’ll work with you. A part fails in 30 days? Also covered.
