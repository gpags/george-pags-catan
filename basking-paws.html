Common questions

Will it really be my cat?

Yes. Every Basking Paws is painted to match your cat. Right after checkout, upload a photo on your order page, or reply to your confirmation email with it. We match colors and markings as closely as the art style allows, and simplify very complex patterns.

What do I get if I buy more than one?

Buy 2: off the second, plus a free keychain for every cat. Buy 3: free shipping too. It’s all applied automatically in your cart.

How long does it take?

1–2 business days to make once your photo arrives, then 3–5 business days in the mail. We email you photo and video updates along the way.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Tick “This is a gift”, add a note if you like, and use their address at checkout. We leave prices and the receipt out of the box.

Does it take the same pads?

Yes. Every Cat Scratchers frame takes the same cardboard pad, on purpose.

What if it breaks?

The cardboard is meant to get shredded — that is the consumable part. Damaged on arrival? Tell us within 7 days and we replace it. A part fails in 30 days? Also covered. Details.
